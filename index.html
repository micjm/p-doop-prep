Recommended prep

Conditional chains multiply — that is what conditional probability means, so a child set to 0 while its parent is 0 changes nothing. The artificial intelligence chain is Joe Carlsmith's with his defaults. The war, biology, grid and climate chains are this site's decomposition; no source published them as chains. Premise values are judgments, not measurements. The "likely to matter" percentages and their action×risk weights come from this site's synthesis of the research notes (Ready.gov, Finland 72h, Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security, Boyd & Wilson 2023, Todd, Zvi, Levin, 80,000 Hours) — no published source ranks household actions by cost-effectiveness, so treat these as a ranking aid, not a measurement.
